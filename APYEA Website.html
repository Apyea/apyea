About APYEA

Who we are

The Andhra Pradesh Yoga Educators Association (APYEA) is a professional association established to provide a common platform for qualified Yoga professionals across Andhra Pradesh.

The Association seeks to bring together professionals working in different areas of Yoga, including:

  • Yoga Teachers
  • Yoga Educators
  • Yoga Instructors
  • Yoga Practitioners
  • Yoga Researchers
  • Yoga Trainers
  • Academicians

APYEA aims to strengthen professional unity while encouraging knowledge sharing, collaboration, continuing education, ethical practice, research, and professional excellence.

Our background

The growing acceptance of Yoga in education, healthcare, wellness, sports, workplaces, and community development has created an increasing demand for professionally qualified Yoga educators.

At the same time, Yoga professionals come from diverse educational backgrounds and institutions, with qualifications ranging from certificate and diploma programmes to postgraduate and doctoral degrees.

APYEA provides a common professional platform through which qualified Yoga educators can collectively contribute to the development of Yoga education and professional standards in Andhra Pradesh.

Our objectives

  • Promote appropriate employment and professional opportunities

    For qualified Yoga professionals in government, educational, healthcare, institutional, private, and other relevant sectors, based on recognized qualifications and applicable requirements.

  • Engage with the Government and relevant authorities

    Regarding appropriate qualification criteria and recruitment rules for Yoga Teacher and Yoga Instructor positions.

  • Establish professional networks and forums

    For communication, collaboration, knowledge sharing, and professional interaction among Yoga educators, practitioners, researchers, and other professionals.

  • Support career progression and professional development

    Of Yoga Teachers and Educators through appropriate policies, training, continuing education, and skill development.

  • Build collaboration

    Among academic institutions, Yoga organizations, government bodies, healthcare institutions, spiritual organizations, and other relevant organizations for spreading the benefits of Yoga Education.

  • Promote professional development and capacity building in Yoga and allied subjects

    Through training programmes, workshops, seminars, conferences, continuing education, and other professional development initiatives for Yoga teachers, educators, and practitioners.

  • Promote the integration of Yoga education

    Within educational institutions and appropriate academic and developmental programmes, including schools, colleges, universities, and other learning environments.

  • Promote, propagate, and popularize Yoga

    Throughout society for physical, mental, emotional, spiritual, and holistic well-being.

  • Promote research, innovation, and evidence-informed Yoga education

    While respecting the traditional foundations and the broader Indian knowledge systems of Yoga.

Our vision

To establish a strong, united, respected, and professionally recognized community of Yoga Teachers and Yoga Educators, contributing to the advancement of Yoga education, professional excellence, and holistic well-being in Andhra Pradesh and beyond.

Our mission

APYEA's mission is to:

  • Unite qualified Yoga professionals on a common professional platform.
  • Promote excellence in Yoga teaching and education, and encourage continuing professional development and lifelong learning.
  • Strengthen the professional identity and dignity of Yoga educators through appropriate standards and qualification frameworks.
  • Encourage research and academic development in Yoga, and build partnerships with government, academic, healthcare, and Yoga institutions.
  • Create awareness about the authentic, responsible, and systematic practice and teaching of Yoga.

Why APYEA

Why do we need APYEA?

Yoga is expanding rapidly across education, healthcare, wellness, sports, workplaces, and community programmes. This growth creates an important need for qualified, competent, and professionally recognized Yoga educators.

However, Yoga professionals come from different educational institutions and qualification pathways, and opportunities for professional interaction, career development, employment, and collective representation may remain fragmented.

APYEA aims to bridge this gap by bringing qualified Yoga professionals together on one common platform.
